Our story

A part of Scandinavia, plated in Singapore.

OVERBRØD is a Scandinavian inspired deli focusing on Smørrebrød (Danish open-faced sandwiches) and other Scandinavian dishes like Swedish meatballs and Norwegian reindeer stew. Using techniques like curing, smoking, pickling, they aim to bring a part of Scandinavia to Singapore but with an OVERBRØD twist.
