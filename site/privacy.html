This policy explains how OVERBRØD ("we", "us") collects, uses, discloses and protects personal data in accordance with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

What we collect

When you request a table through this website, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, reservation date/time, party size, and any notes you add (e.g. allergies or occasion).

Why we collect it (purpose)

  • To receive, manage and confirm your reservation.
  • To contact you about your booking (e.g. to confirm, reschedule or if there's an issue).

We will not use your data for any other purpose without your consent, and we do not sell your personal data.

Consent

By ticking the consent box and sending a booking request, you consent to the above. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (below); we'll stop using your data for those purposes, though it may affect our ability to manage your booking.

How your booking is sent

Submitting the booking form opens your own email application with the booking details pre-filled, addressed to us. Your details are transmitted through your email provider when you press send.
